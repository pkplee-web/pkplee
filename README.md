## 📂 Featured Projects

### 📊 Investor Sentiment & Apple Stock During COVID-19
**Python · pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn**

Analyzed StockTwits investor sentiment alongside Apple (AAPL) market data to explore whether online discussion activity led or followed market movements during the COVID-19 crash.

- Cleaned and combined social sentiment and market datasets.
- Investigated relationships among bullish/bearish posts, stock activity, and trading volume.
- Found a stronger relationship between posting activity and **same-day trading volume** than evidence of sentiment consistently leading prices.

**Repository:** (https://github.com/pkplee-web/stocktwits-aapl-analysis)
